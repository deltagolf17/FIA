Shift cycle
Each platoon (A, B, C, D) works an 8-day cycle:
Day 1 = D1 (1st day shift) · Day 2 = D2 (2nd day shift)
Day 3 = N1 (1st night shift) · Day 4 = N2 (2nd night shift)
Days 5–8 = Off

N2 falls 5 days before the following D1. This is the prior shift window — the reliever must work this shift at their home station (Scenario A) or current relief station (Scenario B) before the relief commences.
